Konso tracks the fuel consumption and the running costs of your cars. It works offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Features:
* Fill-ups: distance, litres and amount give L/100 km, cost per 100 km and price per litre.
* Receipt scanning: take a photo of a fuel receipt and the form fills itself. Text recognition (Tesseract) runs on the phone: photos never leave the device. Tuned for French receipts; always check the values.
* Purchases: oil, parts, tyres, servicing… sorted by category.
* Service reminders: every year or every 10,000 km, whichever comes first (adjustable per car).
* Overview: averages, consumption chart, monthly spending.
* Station prices (France, optional): a map of the stations around you with the price of your fuel, from cheapest to most expensive, and follow as many stations as you like, with a chart of the price.
* Several cars, each with its own history and reminders.
* Automatic backup to a plain konso.json file, in the folder of your choice.
* 10 languages, light and dark themes, built-in help.

Privacy: the network permission serves the app's own files on 127.0.0.1; every request to any other host is blocked. The one exception, off by default: if you turn it on in the Stations tab, the app may reach the official French fuel price site (data.economie.gouv.fr) and, for the map background, OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), and only those. The location permission is only used by the map's "Locate me" button: your position stays on the phone.

Konso was built with AI (Claude Code); every version is tested on a real phone by a human. Its source code is open, under the GPL-3.0 licence.
